In plain terms

What a psychological report actually does

A report doesn't argue the facts of your case — your lawyer does that. What it does is give the court a fuller picture of you: what led up to the offence, what's changed since, and how you're placed to move forward. Depending on your matter, that can support the court in considering a Section 10 outcome (no conviction recorded), a Section 9B outcome, or — in some cases — a Section 14 mental health diversion before the matter even proceeds.

Confidential & respectful

The assessment is a normal, one-on-one clinical conversation — about your background, what happened, and what's changed since. There's no right or wrong way to answer, and it's not an interrogation: it's there to give the court context alongside your lawyer's own case.

Frequently asked, by clients & families

Before you get in touch

Will a report guarantee a certain result?
No. A report is one piece of evidence the court may consider alongside everything else in your matter — your lawyer can advise how it fits your case. Nothing on this page is legal advice or a guarantee of outcome.
Is the assessment confidential?
Yes — it's treated like any clinical psychology appointment. What goes into the report is limited to what's relevant for the court, and it's prepared with your circumstances in mind, not to catch you out.
What does it cost?
A standard report is $2,000, usually ready within 10 business days. If your court date is close, a five-business-day urgent service is available for $2,500.
I don't have a lawyer yet — can I still get in touch?
Yes, you're welcome to reach out directly with questions. It's best to also have a solicitor acting for you, since they'll advise on whether and how a report fits your matter.
What should I bring to the assessment?
Any paperwork from police or the court — the facts sheet, charges, and criminal history if relevant — helps make the assessment more efficient. Your lawyer can usually provide this.
Where do assessments happen?
In person at the Randwick practice (373 Avoca St, Randwick NSW 2031); other arrangements can be discussed if you're unable to attend in person.
